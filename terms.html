Terms of Use

Last updated: November 16, 2026

These are the terms for using Underbook: the browser extension, Underbook for Safari, the web price checker and the price-drop emails. They are short because Underbook does one thing: it shows you what other booking sites list for the stay you are looking at.

Free, with no account

Underbook is free to use. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. A price-drop alert needs only the email address you choose to give, and every alert email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Underbook books nothing

Underbook does not book rooms and does not take any payment. When you open a booking site from Underbook, you book on that site, with that site, under its own terms and its own cancellation rules. Any question about a booking, a payment or a refund is for the site you booked on or the hotel.

Prices are as each site reports them

The prices Underbook shows are the ones each booking site reports for your dates, with taxes and fees as each site reports them; a resort or destination fee may be extra. Prices can change at any time, and a rate shown may already have changed or sold out when you open the site. Underbook makes no promise that any price it shows is still available. Always check the final price on the booking site before you book.

How Underbook earns money

When you open a booking site through Underbook and complete a booking, Underbook may earn a commission from that site, at no extra cost to you. It never changes what Underbook shows. More on this in how Underbook works.

Your data

What Underbook reads, sends and keeps is in the privacy policy.

Contact

Underbook is run by DPWonder Software. Questions? Write to hi@underbook.co.
