Against the other big sites and the hotel's own site

What about Genius and members-only prices?

Our snapshots hold the public prices Google Hotels shows, not signed-in ones. Booking.com says Genius Level 1 gives 10% off stays, applied before taxes and fees (booking.com/genius.html, read October 5, 2026). At 1,736 of 4,729 hotels (37%) another site was already 10% or more below Booking.com's public price, so a 10% member discount would still have left Booking.com at or above that listing; the lower listing named a room at 507 of them.

Members-only prices are real, though. When we opened Booking.com's own pages for 27 hotels from the study on October 2 and 3, 2026, not signed in, 25 showed the price Google Hotels listed within 2%. At the other 2, the page offered a members-only price 10.0% and 9.1% below Google's figure. More: is the Genius discount worth it?

Booking.com in the full price study

Across all 17 snapshots of the hotel price study (7,044 hotel prices in 368 cities, September 22 to October 10, 2026), some site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 5,114 hotels. The lowest of those listings was Booking.com's at 147 of them (2.9%), and it named a room at 122; Super.com had the lowest at 1,340. So Booking.com rarely holds a price well below the going rate, and when it does, it usually says which room.

How to check if Booking.com has the lowest price for your stay

  1. Note Booking.com's total for your room. Open the hotel on Booking.com with your dates and write down the total with taxes and fees for the room you want, signed in if you get a Genius discount. Booking.com's lowest listing named its room at 4,559 of 5,264 hotels (87%), so you know what you are comparing.
  2. Compare it with the other booking sites for the same dates. Look up the same hotel and dates on Google Hotels, which lists the booking sites side by side, and compare totals with totals, or before-tax prices with before-tax prices. Another site listed the stay 5% or more below Booking.com's public price at 2,520 of 4,729 hotels (53%), most often Super.com, Vio.com, Hotels.com or Agoda.
  3. Check the hotel's own site and the room behind any lower price. Open the hotel's own website for the same dates: it was within 1% of Booking.com at 1,648 of 2,965 hotels (56%). If another listing is lower, open it and confirm the room, the cancellation terms and the total before you book; the lower listing named a room at only 688 of the 2,520 (27%).

Booking.com prices: common questions

Does Booking.com have the cheapest hotel prices?+

Usually not. In our 14 price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026, compared like with like, Booking.com's public price was at or below every other booking site's price for the same stay at 846 of 4,729 hotels (18%), and another site was 5% or more lower at 2,520 (53%). It usually matched the other big booking sites, and the lower prices were mostly on smaller sites, often without naming a room.

Google Hotels vs Booking.com: which is cheaper?+

Usually Google Hotels shows a lower price than Booking.com's, because it lists Booking.com beside the other booking sites: in our 14 price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026, with Google Hotels' prices for a US searcher in US dollars, Booking.com's public price was at or below every other booking site's price for the same stay, compared like with like, at only 846 of 4,729 hotels (18%), and another site listed it 5% or more lower at 2,520 (53%). Google Hotels does not take the booking itself: Google says a booking link takes you to the partner's site to book, so open the lower listing and compare the room, the cancellation terms and the total with Booking.com's before you book; at 1,832 of the 2,520 the lower listing named no room.

Is Booking.com cheaper than Expedia?+

Most often the two were the same. At 3,056 hotels where both prices were on the same tax footing, they were within $2 at 1,797 (59%); Expedia was more than 2% lower at 945 (31%) and Booking.com at 268 (9%).

Is Booking.com cheaper than booking direct with the hotel?+

Not usually. At the 2,965 hotels where Google Hotels listed both and the prices could be compared like with like, they were within 1% at 1,648 (56%), Booking.com was more than 1% higher at 878 (30%) and more than 1% lower at 439 (15%).

Why does another site list the same hotel for less than Booking.com?+

In our data the lower listing was on one of the other big booking sites at 596 of the 2,520 hotels where another site was 5% or more below Booking.com, and on a smaller site, often a reseller such as Super.com or Vio.com, at the other 1,924. At 1,832 of the 2,520 the lower listing named no room, and a lower price can also come with stricter cancellation terms. Check the room and the terms before you book.

Does a Genius discount make Booking.com the lowest price?+

Sometimes, not usually. Genius Level 1 gives 10% off stays, before taxes and fees, Booking.com says. At 1,736 of 4,729 hotels (37%) another site was already 10% or more below Booking.com's public price, so a 10% discount would still have left Booking.com at or above it; that lower listing named a room at 507 of them. How was this measured?+

From the hotel price study: in 14 snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026 we recorded every booking site's price that Google Hotels showed a US searcher, in US dollars, for a two-night stay checking in between October 30 and November 5, 2026, at 5,830 hotels in 348 cities. Each site's lowest price per hotel; before-tax prices compared with before-tax prices and all-in with all-in, as each hotel's two going rates show; hotels where that could not be read were left out.
