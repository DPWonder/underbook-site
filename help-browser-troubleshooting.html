Troubleshooting by browser

If the banner never appears, start here. On any browser, first open a hotel page with your dates picked and reload it, then click the Underbook icon, which checks the page you are on.

Underbook does nothing in Chrome. What should I check?

Check that Underbook is turned on and allowed on all sites: right-click its icon (or click the three dots next to it in the puzzle-piece menu), choose "This can read and change site data", pick "On all sites", then reload the hotel page.

  • Turned on: open chrome://extensions and make sure the switch next to Underbook is on.
  • Site access: with "When you click the extension", the banner appears only after you click the icon.
  • Incognito windows: Chrome runs extensions there only if you allow it: chrome://extensions, Details under Underbook, "Allow in Incognito".
  • Tabs opened before the install: reload them.
  • Can't find the icon: it is under the puzzle-piece menu until you pin it.
  • On a phone, Chrome does not run Underbook: use the web price checker.

Underbook does nothing in Microsoft Edge. What should I check?

Check that Underbook is turned on and allowed on all sites: open edge://extensions, choose Details under Underbook, set "Site access" to "On all sites", then reload the hotel page.

  • Turned on: the switch next to Underbook on edge://extensions.
  • InPrivate windows: turn on "Allow in InPrivate" on the same Details page.
  • The icon: Extensions (the puzzle piece), then the eye icon next to Underbook, to show it in the toolbar.
  • Tabs opened before the install: reload them.
  • Your version: the Details page shows it. Edge Add-ons and the Chrome Web Store each update Underbook on their own schedule, so the two can serve different versions.

Underbook does nothing in Firefox. What should I check?

Check that you have Firefox 140 or newer on a computer and that Underbook may access your data for all websites: open about:addons, choose Underbook, turn that permission on in its permissions tab, then reload the hotel page.

  • Turned on: about:addons lists Underbook as enabled.
  • Private windows: on the same page, set "Run in Private Windows" to Allow.
  • The icon: from version 0.5.33, Underbook puts its button on the toolbar when you install it; otherwise use the Extensions button (the puzzle piece) to pin it.
  • Usage statistics are optional in Firefox: allowing technical and interaction data, or not, changes nothing about the price checks.
  • Firefox for Android 142 or newer: add Underbook from Firefox Add-ons. It is not a toolbar button there: on a hotel page where the card does not appear by itself, open Firefox's menu, then Extensions, then Underbook.

How do I remove Underbook?

Right-click the Underbook icon and choose "Remove from Chrome", "Remove from Microsoft Edge" or, in Firefox, "Remove Extension"; you can also remove it from chrome://extensions, edge://extensions or about:addons.

  • Everything it stored on your device goes with it, and there is no account to close.
  • A price watch you set keeps running, because watches run on Underbook's service: click Unsubscribe in its email to stop it (how to stop alerts).
  • Your browser then opens a short page on underbook.co that asks why you left; answering is optional.
  • To keep it but quiet it for a while, turn it off with its switch on the same extensions page instead.
