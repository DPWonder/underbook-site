Type the hotel and your dates. You will see every booking site's price, whether a cheaper one names the same room, and where free cancellation costs no more.

What it tells you

  • The going rate for your dates, the price most booking sites agree on, with taxes and fees included when the sites publish them.
  • The lowest listed price and the site that has it, with a link straight to it.
  • Whether it is the same room. A lower price often comes with a different room or different cancellation terms. Underbook says "same room name" only when the room names match, and "check the room" when they do not. Why that matters, measured on 7,044 hotel prices.
  • When no site we checked is 5% or more below the site you are on, it says so, so you can book without opening ten tabs.

Good to know

  • It stays quiet on pages that are not hotel pages, with two small exceptions: on a booking site's home page or hotel search results it may show one short tip (at most one a day and once every 14 days per site, never again after "Don't show tips"), and right after you book on Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com or Agoda it offers, once, to watch that booking.
  • If it does not appear on a hotel you are looking at, click the Underbook icon in your toolbar to check that page. Pin it first: click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to Underbook.
  • It never books anything and never sees payment details. Privacy policy.
