Before you book on a site you do not know

  1. Who runs it. Find the company name in its terms or site footer. Each section below says what we found, and when we read it.
  2. Who you book with. Many of these sites act as an agent or intermediary: the hotel or another provider sells the room, and its terms decide refunds and changes.
  3. The room. A listing that does not name the room may be for the smallest or a non-refundable one. Compare the room name and bed type with the hotel's own site.
  4. The all-in total and the cancellation terms. Compare the price after taxes and fees on the final booking screen, and read the cancellation line before you pay. See whether free cancellation is worth it.
