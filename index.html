What is Underbook?

Underbook is a free browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and a free web price checker. For the hotel and dates you are looking at, it shows the price each booking site lists on Google Hotels, the going rate (the most common price among the listings that name a room or offer free cancellation), and whether a lower listing names the same room. It never books for you: you book on the site you choose.

Where it works
On a computer, on hotel pages at Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Agoda and hotels' own sites. On a phone, use the web price checker.
What it costs
Nothing, and there is no account. We may earn a commission when you book through our links, at no extra cost to you. It never changes which prices we show.
What it sends
The hotel, your dates and the number of adults, to look up prices (the full list). Never your identity, your browsing history or your payment details.
What it does not do
Compare a site that Google Hotels does not list, or call a room the same when the room names do not match.

Add it to Chrome, Edge or Firefox. To check our work: how Underbook works and our price study.

Radically honest about it.

Most hotel sites hide the fine print. We do the opposite: here's exactly what we will and won't do.

What we promise

  • ✅ We never see your payment info: you book on the site you pick.
  • ✅ We tell you whether the cheaper listing names the same room, and say so plainly when it doesn't.
  • ✅ When no site we checked is 5% or more below the site you are on, we say so in one line.

What we don't do

  • 🚫 We don't book for you or hold your card.
  • 🚫 We don't pretend an unfamiliar rate is the same room: when the room names don't match, we say so and tell you to check before booking.
  • 🚫 We don't make up price gaps to get your click.
  • 🚫 We don't sell your data.

Frequently asked

Why is the same room cheaper on a site I've never heard of?+

Lesser-known booking sites buy hotel rooms through different wholesale channels and spend less on advertising, so their listed rates are sometimes lower. Often not for a room it names, though, which is exactly why Underbook tells you whether the cheaper listing names the same room.

How do I know it's really the same room?+

We compare the room names the booking sites publish. When the cheaper site names a room that matches, we mark it “same room name”: strong evidence, not proof. When it names no room or a different one, we say so plainly, so you can check the room and cancellation terms before you book. We never assume.

Why doesn't Google Hotels just show me this?+

Google does list some of these sites, but they're buried below the sponsored results most people click. Underbook checks them for the exact stay you're looking at and brings the result to you.

How does Underbook make money?+

If you book through a site we surface, we may earn a small commission from that site. You pay nothing extra. We'll show you the lower rate even when our commission would be smaller. The comparison is for you, not us.

Is my data safe?+

When you open a hotel page, Underbook sends the hotel name, your dates and your party size to its price service to look up current rates. Nothing that identifies you goes with it. We never see or store your payment details, booking happens on the site you choose, and we don't sell your data.

Is Underbook available yet?+

Yes. Underbook is a free extension on the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons. Add it and it checks the next hotel page you open. On a phone, the web price checker runs the same check with no install.
