For independent hotels

Is another site selling your rooms for less than you are?

In September 2026 we priced 1,142 independent hotels across the United States on every booking site Google Hotels lists. At 812 of them (71%), at least one site showed the same stay more than 5% below the rate most sites agreed on. The site most often lowest was Super.com.

A guest who sees that lower price books there instead of with you, or asks you to match it at the desk. Checking every site for every date by hand takes hours, so most of it goes unseen.

The weekly report

  • One email every Monday. For your next four weekends, or the dates you pick, every site that lists your hotel and the price it shows, with and without taxes and fees.
  • The listings below your rate, marked. Each one more than 5% below your own direct rate, with the site, the room name it shows (or that it shows none), and how far below it is.
  • What changed since last week. Sites that started or stopped undercutting you, so you know whom to call.
  • Nothing to install or connect. No login, no channel manager access, no code on your site. We check the public prices a guest sees.
