How many sites Google Hotels listed

Across our 14 price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026, we recorded every seller Google Hotels listed at 5,830 hotels in 348 cities. Half of the hotels had between 11 and 22 booking sites listed and the median was 17; 4,683 (80%) had 10 or more, and 353 had fewer than 5. Counting each hotel's own site, 4,519 different sellers appeared.

Does Google Hotels miss deals?

It found plenty of lower prices, mostly on smaller sites. Across all 17 snapshots of our study (7,044 hotel prices), some site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate (the most common price among listings that named a room or offered free cancellation) at 5,114 (73%). The lowest of those prices was on Booking.com, Hotels.com, Expedia, Priceline or Agoda at only 648 (13%); Super.com alone had it at 1,340 and Vio.com at 535. Those lower listings named the room at 820 of the 5,114 (16%), so a lower price is not always the same room: see same room, different price.

What it leaves out: Hotwire did not appear at any of the 5,830 hotels; its Hot Rates hide the hotel until you pay. Our snapshots recorded the public prices without signing in, so member prices a site shows only after you log in are not in these counts either.

How we measured: in each snapshot we recorded every booking site's price that Google Hotels showed a US searcher, in US dollars, for a two-night stay checking in between October 30 and November 5, 2026, at 5,830 hotels in 348 cities. For each hotel we took each site's lowest price, all-in with taxes and fees where the site reported them, otherwise its listed rate. Our snapshot kept up to 28 listings per hotel; 788 hotels reached that limit, so for them Google Hotels may have listed more sites than we counted. A price we saw is one moment, not a completed booking. Method and the CSVs: our hotel price study.

Where Google Hotels gets its prices

Google says its hotel prices come from partners: hotel owners, online travel agencies, metasearch sites and other travel providers. Paid booking links carry an "Ads" badge. Google is not the seller: a booking link takes you to the partner's site to book, and questions about the booking go to that partner. For how close its prices are to what you pay, see is Google Hotels accurate?

Some of the partners are metasearch sites themselves, and some belong to the same company and show the same price: Expedia, Orbitz, Travelocity and CheapTickets did in our data, and trivago's booking site priced differently from Expedia (trivago vs Expedia).

Using Google Hotels well

  1. Look past the big names. When some site was 5% or more below the going rate, the lowest price was on one of the five big names only 13% of the time in our data.
  2. Open the lowest one and read it. Check the room name, the total after taxes and fees, and the cancellation terms; see whether free cancellation is worth it.
  3. Check the hotel's own site. It was listed at 66% of hotels; a member rate may only show once you sign in there.
  4. Know who you pay. Before booking on a site you do not know, read our guides: which sites had the lowest prices and how to compare hotel prices.

Google Hotels: common questions

Does Google Hotels show every booking site?+

No, but it shows many. In our snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026, Google Hotels listed a median of 17 booking sites per hotel (half of the 5,830 hotels had between 11 and 22), and 4,519 different sellers in all, counting each hotel's own site. Hotwire did not appear at all.

Does Google Hotels miss some deals?+

It found plenty of lower prices, mostly on smaller sites: some site was 5% or more below the going rate at 5,114 of the 7,044 hotel prices in our study, and the lowest of them was on Booking.com, Hotels.com, Expedia, Priceline or Agoda at only 648. What it did not show in our data: Hotwire's Hot Rates, which hide the hotel until you pay. Our snapshots also recorded public prices without signing in, so a member price a site shows only after you log in is not in our counts.

Does Google Hotels show the hotel's own price?+

Often. The hotel's own site was listed at 3,874 of the 5,830 hotels in our snapshots (66%). A member rate may only show once you sign in on the hotel's site.

Is Hotwire on Google Hotels?+

Not in our data: Hotwire did not appear as a seller at any of the 5,830 hotels. Its Hot Rates hide the hotel's name and exact address until after you pay.

Does Google Hotels sell the room?+

No. Google says its hotel prices come from partners such as hotel owners, online travel agencies and metasearch sites, and clicking a booking link takes you to the partner's site to book. Paid links carry an "Ads" badge.

How do I compare the sites' prices for my hotel?+

Open the hotel on Google Hotels and look past the first few prices, or let Underbook do it: the free browser extension shows booking sites' prices for your hotel and dates side by side, all-in where the sites report taxes and fees, and says whether the cheaper listing names your room. You can also check a hotel on underbook.co without installing anything.
