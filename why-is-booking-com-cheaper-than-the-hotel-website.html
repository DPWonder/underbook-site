  • At chain hotels the two were within 2% at 82%; at independent hotels at only 31%, and there either side could be lower: Booking.com at 23%, the hotel's site at 46%.

So a lower Booking.com price is the exception, not the rule, and at chain hotels it is rare. Where it happens, compare the room and the cancellation terms: a listed price alone does not show them.

Why Booking.com can be lower

The hotel sets the rate. Booking.com's page on how it works says: "The rates displayed on our Platform are set by the Service Providers." So a lower price there is a rate the hotel chose to list on Booking.com, and Booking.com is paid afterwards: "Once your stay is finished, the Service Provider just pays us a commission."

Booking.com can pay for a discount. The same page says: "We may finance rewards or other benefits out of our own pocket." Source: Booking.com, How we work (updated May 31, 2025), read October 25, 2026.

Member prices. "Genius is Booking.com's free loyalty program." Its first level brings "10% discounts on stays", and Booking.com adds: "To see all your available discounts, sign in before you start searching for an option to book." Our figures are the public prices a signed-out visitor gets, so a Genius price can be lower still, and a hotel's own member price too. Source: Booking.com Genius, read October 25, 2026; more in is the Genius discount worth it?

Why the hotel's own site is lower more often

In our data the hotel's site was the lower one 2.0 times as often as Booking.com, mostly at independent hotels. A booking on the hotel's own site carries no booking-site commission (Booking.com's, quoted above, is paid on each stay), which gives a hotel a reason to list its own rate at or below the one it gives a booking site. Booking direct is not always lower, though: compare the two totals for the same room.

How to check before you book

  1. Open both prices for the same room and dates, on Booking.com and on the hotel's own site.
  2. Compare the cancellation terms. A non-refundable rate is a different product from a free-cancellation one.
  3. Compare totals with taxes and fees, not the first price: on US pages Booking.com adds the hotel's taxes as a separate line.
  4. Check a signed-in price on both, Genius on Booking.com and the hotel's member rate, if you have either.

Booking.com and the hotel's site: common questions

Why is Booking.com cheaper than the hotel website?+

Usually it is not: like with like at 3,345 hotel prices in our 16 price snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026, the hotel's own site was more than 2% lower at 877 (26%) and Booking.com at 439 (13%). When Booking.com is lower, Booking.com says the hotel sets the rates it shows and that it may pay for rewards or other benefits itself.

Is it cheaper to book direct or on Booking.com?+

At chain hotels the two were within 2% at 82% of hotels in our data. At independent hotels the gap is wider in both directions, and the hotel's own site was lower more often (46% against 23%). Compare the totals for the same room and terms.

Who sets the price on Booking.com?+

The hotel. Booking.com's page on how it works says the rates displayed on its platform are set by the service providers, and that the provider pays Booking.com a commission once the stay is finished.

Is the lower Booking.com price the same room?+

Not always. Where Booking.com was lower than the hotel's site, its listing named the room at 370 of 439 hotels. Open both, match the room name, bed type and cancellation terms, then compare.

Can the hotel's own site be lower than Booking.com?+

Yes, and in our data it was, more often: more than 2% lower at 26% of hotels, typically by 10%. Member prices on either side can be lower again.
