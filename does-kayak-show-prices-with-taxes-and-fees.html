What KAYAK says, and what we saw

Each line with its source and the date we read it.

What KAYAK says it shows

Its guide to resort fees lists two displays: "The total stay, including taxes and fees (which is the default)" and "Nightly total, including taxes and fees". On resort fees: "Most resorts will list their fees on their websites and you’ll see these fees listed on KAYAK too."

KAYAK, What is a resort fee (published May 26, 2025), read October 25, 2026.

What we saw on its pages

On September 30, 2026 (a November 4 to 6 stay, 2 adults, US dollars), 7 of the 8 pages led with "Nightly including fees - Before taxes" and 1 with "Stay total without taxes". The price matched the one Google listed, to within $1, on all 8.

Our check of 8 pages at 5 hotels, September 30, 2026.

What KAYAK charges

"KAYAK doesn’t charge you any extra fees. Prices come straight from the travel sites to your screen." And: "Providers set and sell these prices, and the final price is confirmed when you book."

KAYAK help, Search and discovery, read October 25, 2026.

Are KAYAK's prices accurate?

On the pages we opened, yes: when the offer was still listed, all 8 KAYAK, momondo and HotelsCombined prices matched the price Google listed, to within $1, about three hours after our study priced those hotels. At 3 of the 8 hotels we checked, though, KAYAK was no longer listed by then.

The lower price was the metasearch's own bookable rate, the one under the KAYAK or HotelsCombined logo. The booking sites named on the same card were $7 to $28 a night higher, so choosing one of them from that card cost more.

KAYAK's own help says: "A price you see on KAYAK may no longer be available by the time you click through." And: "Occasionally, problems arise in the price and availability data we receive from travel sites, which can cause mismatches." Source: KAYAK help, Search and discovery, read October 25, 2026.

What the US rule asks

The FTC's fee rule covers anyone who shows US hotel prices: "The Rule covers any business that offers, displays, or advertises live-event tickets or short-term lodging, including third-party platforms, resellers, and travel agents." A mandatory resort fee must be in the total shown first, while "taxes or other government charges" may be added later, and "The final amount of payment must be displayed as prominently as, or more prominently than, the total price." Source: FTC, Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees: Frequently Asked Questions, read October 25, 2026.

So a before-tax price is allowed in the US as long as mandatory fees are in it and the taxes appear before you pay. The number to compare between sites is the final amount. More: which booking sites show resort fees upfront.

How to see the full KAYAK price

  1. Switch the price display to the total stay including taxes and fees in KAYAK's price drop-down, if your page shows a before-tax price.
  2. Check whose rate it is: KAYAK's own rate, or a booking site or hotel listed under it.
  3. Open it and read the last screen on the site that takes the booking: the final amount, and any fee paid at the property.
  4. Compare that total with the same room on one other site before you pay.

KAYAK prices: common questions

Does KAYAK show prices with taxes and fees?+

Its guide calls the total stay including taxes and fees its default display, but all 8 KAYAK, momondo and HotelsCombined hotel pages we opened on September 30, 2026 led with a price before taxes (7 nightly, 1 a stay total). Switch the display to the total, or read the total on the booking site before you pay.

Does KAYAK charge booking fees?+

KAYAK says it does not charge you any extra fees and that prices come straight from the travel sites. The site that takes your booking sets the final price, taxes and any fee paid at the property.

Are KAYAK prices accurate?+

In our check, yes when the offer was still there: 8 of 8 KAYAK, momondo and HotelsCombined prices matched the price Google listed, about three hours after we first priced those hotels. KAYAK itself says a price may no longer be available by the time you click through.

Why does the KAYAK price go up when I click through?+

A price shown before tax rises when the taxes are added, and KAYAK says a price may no longer be available by the time you click through. A resort fee or a different room can also change the total.

Is it safe to book through KAYAK?+

KAYAK's help says: "Either way, the booking is with the provider, not with KAYAK." So check whose rate you are paying for, KAYAK's own or a site listed under it, and read that site's cancellation terms before you pay.
