When Directo may be enough

If you want negotiated rates on its own booking platform, or the extension with far more users and ratings, Directo does that job, free and with no sign-up. Underbook is the one to try if you want every site Google Hotels lists for your stay, small sites and the hotel's own site included, want to be told whether a lower price names the same room, or want an email if a refundable stay drops after you book.

What to look for in any of them

Three things from our hotel price study (7,044 hotel prices in 368 cities, 17 snapshots) that decide whether a price comparison is worth trusting:

  1. Which sites it checks. Some site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 5,114 of 7,044 hotels (73%), and the lowest of those listings was on Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline or Agoda at only 641 of 5,114 (12.5%). Most lower prices sit on smaller sites, so the number of sites checked matters.
  2. What it says about the room. At 4,294 of 5,114 hotels (84%) with a lower listing, the lowest named no room at all. A tool can only match a room the listing names, so look for one that tells you when it cannot.
  3. Taxes. At 2,542 of 3,371 hotels (75%) where taxes added 10% or more (snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026), the listings mixed before-tax and all-in prices, and at each of those the lowest listing was a before-tax price. Compare all-in totals, or know which you are seeing.

Directo alternatives: common questions

What is a good alternative to Directo?+

Underbook, RatePunk and Hotel Ninja are free extensions that also compare a hotel's price across booking sites on the page you are viewing. Underbook installs on Chrome, Edge and Firefox, checks every booking site Google Hotels lists for your stay, says whether a lower price names the same room, and has Underbook for Safari, a free app for iPhone and iPad on the App Store since November 10, 2026 (iOS 16 or later) and a web price checker for other phones.

Is Directo free?+

Yes, with no sign-up, by its own site, read November 8, 2026. It may earn affiliate commissions, its Chrome Web Store listing says. Underbook is free too and may also earn a commission if you book through its link.

Does Directo work on Firefox?+

It has a Firefox Add-ons listing, updated October 23, 2026, though its site names Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera as the browsers it works on, read November 8, 2026. Underbook has listings on the Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons.

Is there a Directo app for a phone?+

Its site names a beta for Safari on iPhone and says the full experience is on a computer. Underbook has Underbook for Safari, a free app for iPhone and iPad on the App Store since November 10, 2026 (iOS 16 or later); on any other phone its web price checker runs its check in the browser with no install.

Why try a smaller extension?+

Underbook has far fewer users: 71 on Chrome and no ratings yet, against Directo's 400,000 and 4.5 from 370 ratings, read November 8, 2026. What it adds is a check of every site Google Hotels lists and a plain answer on whether the room matches. You can test that with no install at the web price checker, then decide.
