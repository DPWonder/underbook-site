How it works

  1. 1

    Check your stay

    Type the hotel and your dates into the price checker. You see the price on every booking site for that stay.

  2. 2

    Tell us what you paid

    Tick "I already booked this stay". Enter the price you paid per night, with taxes and fees, and the last day you can cancel for free. Then your email.

  3. 3

    We check once a day

    If a listed rate is at least 5% lower than what you paid on two daily checks in a row, we email you once. You get the site and the price, so you can rebook before your deadline.

What we promise, and what we don't

  • At least 5% lower, on two daily checks in a row.

    One low reading can be noise, so we wait for a second day. The rate must also be at least $5 a night lower (or about that in your currency). Smaller moves are not worth a rebook.

  • Only for bookings you can cancel for free.

    Rebooking only helps if you can cancel the old booking for free. If your rate is non-refundable, here is what you can do instead. Not sure it was worth paying for free cancellation? Refundable vs non-refundable, with our numbers.

  • Check the room before you rebook.

    A lower rate can be for another room, bed type or meal plan. Our email says when we cannot tell. Read the room and the cancellation terms of the new rate on the booking site.

  • You rebook. We never book or cancel anything.

    Book the new rate first. Cancel your old booking only after the new one is confirmed, and before your free cancellation ends. How to rebook, step by step.

  • One alert at most.

    You get a confirmation when you set it up, and at most one alert. We stop checking after your free-cancellation date, or at check-in if you leave the date empty. If nothing drops, we send nothing more.

  • A last-minute drop can come too late.

    We need two checks in a row. A rate that first drops on your deadline day itself comes too late for an alert.

Checked in November 2026

When a lower price turns up, is it real? Mostly yes, for the price. In a check on November 5, 2026, 17 of the 20 lower prices Underbook showed for a Christmas stay were the same price on the booking site about 40 hours later (within 5%, and within 1% at all 17); 1 was no longer offered (Super.com); 1 page could not be read (Vio.com); 1 was no longer offered (Traveluro). What varied more was the room and the cancellation terms.

Free cancellation is what lets you rebook, and the lower listings often do not show it. For Thanksgiving week 2026 (November 25 to 29, priced November 3, 2026), another site listed the same hotel and dates 5% or more below Booking.com at 74 of 76 hotels we compared, a median 22%, about $40 a night. The lowest-priced of those other listings named a room at 3 of the 74 and showed free cancellation at 6; Booking.com's listing at those hotels named a room at 59 and showed free cancellation at 38. Read the room and the cancellation line of the new rate before you rebook.

What we keep

Your email, the hotel, your dates and party size, the price you paid with its currency and the market we price it in, your free-cancellation date, and the booking site's name when the extension sends it. Nothing else about your booking.

We never ask for your booking number, your name or your payment details, and we don't want them. Please don't type them in.

We delete the record the day after your check-out. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe that deletes it at once. Read the privacy policy.
