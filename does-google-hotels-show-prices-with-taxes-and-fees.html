What Google says

Quoted from Google's own pages, read October 27, 2026.

What Google shows

For the United States and Canada: "Google will prominently display the total price, which includes the base rate and mandatory non-governmental fees."

Google Hotel Center, Taxes and Fees Policy, read October 27, 2026.

What partners must send

Partners agree to Google's Price Accuracy Policy: "This policy requires that prices they send to Google must include taxes and fees, and must match the total price you pay on their booking page." Google asks you to "check the final cost of your room carefully".

Google Travel Help, Search for hotels on Google, read October 27, 2026.

Whose price on Google Hotels was all-in

In our 16 price snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026 (6,620 hotel prices, searched as a US traveler), the all-in going rate was a median 11.4% above the before-tax one (12.6% at 3,231 US hotel prices), and 10% or more above at 3,813 of 6,620 (58%). How to read a Google Hotels price

  1. Check whether the view is a nightly price or a total for your stay.
  2. Compare sellers like with like: a metasearch price before tax can look lower than an all-in one.
  3. Open the lowest listing and read the final amount on its last screen.
  4. Look for a resort or destination fee listed as paid at the property.

Google Hotels taxes and fees: common questions

Does Google Hotels show prices with taxes and fees?+

Mostly. Google says partners must send prices that include taxes and fees and match the total on their booking page, and that in the US and Canada it shows a total with the base rate and mandatory non-governmental fees. In our 16 snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026, the big booking sites' prices looked all-in at 91% or more of hotels where taxes added 10% or more.

Why is the price higher when I click through from Google Hotels?+

Some sellers list a price before tax: KAYAK's looked before tax at 37% of hotels with 10% or more in taxes in our snapshots. A price can also change between the search and the booking site, and a resort fee may be paid at the property.

Are Google Hotels prices accurate?+

Google says partners agree to a price accuracy policy and asks you to check the final cost of your room carefully. Read the total on the booking site's last screen before you pay.

How much do taxes add to a hotel price?+

In our snapshots, the all-in going rate was a median 11.4% above the before-tax rate (12.6% at US hotels), and 10% or more at 58% of hotel prices.

Does Underbook show prices with taxes and fees?+

It shows each price all-in where the sites report taxes and fees (a resort or destination fee may be extra), and when no site reports a before-tax rate it says fees may be missing.
