Price spread for the same stay

The same hotel, the same dates, every booking site Google Hotels lists.

  1. 1. Underbook recorded 7,044 hotel prices in 368 cities on every booking site Google Hotels lists, in 17 snapshots from September 22 to October 10, 2026, and at 5,114 of them (73%) a site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate, the most common price among listings that name a room or offer free cancellation.

    Source: Hotel price study (underbook.co/hotel-price-dispersion-study)

  2. 2. In every one of Underbook's 17 snapshots (September 22 to October 10, 2026, 7,044 hotel prices), 61% to 81% of hotels had some site listing the stay 5% or more below the going rate.

    Source: Hotel price study (underbook.co/hotel-price-dispersion-study)

  3. 3. Of the 5,114 hotel prices with a listing 5% or more below the going rate in Underbook's 17 snapshots (September 22 to October 10, 2026), the lowest listing named no room at 4,294 (84%), so whether the lower price buys the same room was usually unknown.

    Source: Are hotel prices the same on every site? (underbook.co/hotel-price-parity-myth)

  4. 4. Where Underbook compared room names (14 snapshots, September 25 to October 1, 2026, 5,830 priced hotels), no listing 5% or more below the going rate named a room at all at 2,505 of the 4,181 hotels that had such a listing (59.9%).

    Source: Hotel price study (underbook.co/hotel-price-dispersion-study)

  5. 5. When Underbook priced the same stay again a week later (September 22 and 29, 2026, 181 hotels priced both times), 121 of the 142 hotels that had a listing 5% or more below the going rate still had one (85%), but the same site was lowest at only 53 (37%).

    Source: Hotel price study (underbook.co/hotel-price-dispersion-study)

Booking sites against the hotel's own site

The hotel's own site is the seller Google Hotels lists under the hotel's exact name.

  1. 6. At the 2,965 hotels where Google Hotels listed both Booking.com and the hotel's own site, Underbook found the two within 1% at 1,648 (56%), Booking.com more than 1% higher at 878 (30%) and more than 1% lower at 439 (15%), compared like with like (14 snapshots, September 25 to October 1, 2026).

    Source: Is it cheaper to book direct with the hotel? (underbook.co/booking-com-vs-hotel-direct)

  2. 7. At chain hotels, Underbook found the hotel's own site within 1% of the lowest price on the six big booking sites (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline, Agoda and Trip.com) at 1,291 of 1,791 hotels (72%) (14 snapshots, September 25 to October 1, 2026).

    Source: Is it cheaper to book direct with the hotel? (underbook.co/booking-com-vs-hotel-direct)

  3. 8. At independent hotels, one of those six big booking sites was lower than the hotel's own site at 810 of 1,507 (54%), and the hotel's own site was lower at 404 (27%), in Underbook's 14 snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026.

    Source: Is it cheaper to book direct with the hotel? (underbook.co/booking-com-vs-hotel-direct)

  4. 9. Compared like with like at 3,345 hotel prices (16 snapshots, September 25 to October 10, 2026), Underbook found the hotel's own site more than 2% below Booking.com at 877 (26%) and Booking.com more than 2% below the hotel's own site at 439 (13%).

    Source: Why is Booking.com cheaper than the hotel's website? (underbook.co/why-is-booking-com-cheaper-than-the-hotel-website)

The method

How we measure

Underbook records the price every booking site lists on Google Hotels for one hotel and the same dates, for 2 adults, as a traveler in the US, in US dollars: a two-night stay in the hotel price study, four nights for the holiday weeks. The going rate is the price the largest number of listings that name a room or offer free cancellation agree on, with ties broken low, and a lower listing is one 5% or more below it. Two sites' prices are compared like with like, before tax against before tax and all-in against all-in. These are prices from one moment, not completed bookings; a site Google Hotels does not list is not in the data, and member or signed-in prices are not in it. The checks of booking-site pages open each page from Google Hotels' own link, signed out. Each source page gives its full method and limits.
