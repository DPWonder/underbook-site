Christmas and New Year's Eve hotel prices, 2026

Are hotels more expensive at Christmas and New Year's Eve? For New Year's Eve, yes at the median hotel we priced, narrowly: on October 13, 2026 its going rate was a median 6.8% higher than for a mid-January week at the same 207 hotels in 25 large US cities. For Christmas, not at the median hotel we priced: its going rate was a median 3.9% lower than mid January at 211 hotels, a difference within chance.

New Year's Eve instead?

On October 13, 2026 we priced the same 225 hotels in 25 large US travel markets three times, on every booking site Google Hotels listed: for Christmas (Wednesday, December 23 to Sunday, December 27, 2026), for New Year's Eve (Tuesday, December 29, 2026 to Saturday, January 2, 2027) and for a mid-January week with the New Year's Eve weekdays (Tuesday, January 12 to Saturday, January 16, 2027), 4 nights each. Two adults, as a traveler in the US, in US dollars. These are one day's prices, about ten weeks ahead; they will move.

The short version

Priced October 13, 2026, at the same hotels: the New Year's Eve going rate was a median 6.8% higher than mid January ($10 a night more; higher at 118 of 207 hotels, lower at 83), and the Christmas going rate was a median 3.9% lower than mid January ($5 a night less; lower at 119 of 211 hotels, higher at 88, a difference within chance). A booking site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 192 of 223 hotels for Christmas and 181 of 217 for New Year's Eve.

  1. 1. Christmas: the going rate was a median 3.9% lower than in mid January, a difference within chance

    211 hotels had a going rate (the rate most booking sites agreed on) for both the Christmas stay (December 23 to 27, 2026) and the January stay (January 12 to 16, 2027). Hotel by hotel, the Christmas rate was a median 3.9% lower (95% interval -8.1% to 0.0%), a median $5 a night less. It was higher at 88 hotels, lower at 119 and the same at 4 (exact sign test, p = 0.037). Before pricing we set p < 0.025 as the bar for a clear difference, since this page makes two comparisons; this one does not, so we do not read the difference as a Christmas effect. The interval does not reach above zero: in this sample there is no sign that the median hotel was priced higher for Christmas than for mid January. Across all hotels with a price, the median going rate was $148 a night for Christmas and $166 for mid January, before taxes and fees. On the all-in going rate, where the sites reported taxes and fees for both stays (211 hotels), the median was 3.0% lower. The two stays share 3 of their 4 nights' weekdays; the Christmas stay has a Saturday night where the January stay has a Tuesday night.

  2. 2. New Year's Eve: the going rate was a median 6.8% higher than in mid January

    207 hotels had a going rate (the rate most booking sites agreed on) for both the New Year's Eve stay (December 29, 2026 to January 2, 2027) and the January stay (January 12 to 16, 2027). Hotel by hotel, the New Year's Eve rate was a median 6.8% higher (95% interval 0.0% to +13.9%), a median $10 a night more. It was higher at 118 hotels, lower at 83 and the same at 6 (exact sign test, p = 0.016). Before pricing we set p < 0.025 as the bar for a clear difference, since this page makes two comparisons; this one clears it, narrowly: with some single cities left out it would not (see how much one city moves it, below). Across all hotels with a price, the median going rate was $178 a night for New Year's Eve and $166 for mid January, before taxes and fees. On the all-in going rate, where the sites reported taxes and fees for both stays (207 hotels), the median was 7.6% higher. The two stays have the same weekdays (Tuesday to Friday nights).

  3. 3. A site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 192 of 223 hotels for Christmas and 181 of 217 for New Year's Eve

    For Christmas, 192 of the 223 hotels with a price (86.1%) had at least one booking site listing the stay 5% or more below the going rate; for New Year's Eve, 181 of 217 (83.4%); for mid January, 169 of 213 (79.3%). Among the hotels priced for both stays, 31 had such a listing only for Christmas and 18 only for mid January (more for Christmas; exact test, p = 0.09, within what chance alone could produce); for New Year's Eve, 27 had such a listing only for New Year's Eve and 20 only for mid January (more for New Year's Eve; exact test, p = 0.38, within what chance alone could produce). For comparison, in the study's 17 snapshots up to October 10, 2026 (2-night stays, cities worldwide), the share ranged from 61% to 81%. Christmas and New Year's Eve were above that range; these are 4-night stays in large US cities, and one snapshot cannot say which of those made the difference.

  4. 4. Where a lower listing existed, the typical gap was about $26 a night for Christmas and $32 for New Year's Eve

    At the 192 Christmas hotels with a listing 5% or more below the going rate, the median gap was 16.9%, about $26 a night before taxes and fees, or about $104 over the 4 nights. For New Year's Eve, at 181 hotels: 18.5%, about $32 a night, about $128 over the stay. For mid January, at 169 hotels: 18.1%, about $29 a night.

  5. 5. Super.com had the lowest price most often for all three stays

    Among the 192 Christmas hotels with a listing 5% or more below the going rate, Super.com had the lowest price most often (93 of 192, 48.4%), then KAYAK.com (27), then Vio.com (21). For New Year's Eve, among 181: Super.com had the lowest price most often (88 of 181, 48.6%), then Vio.com (21), then KAYAK.com (19). For mid January, among 169: Super.com had the lowest price most often (99 of 169, 58.6%), then Hotels In America (11), then Vio.com (10). A site that is lowest at one hotel is often not lowest at the next, so the hotel and dates you want are what to check.

  6. 6. At 178 of 192, the lowest listing for Christmas named no room

    Of the 192 Christmas hotels with a listing 5% or more below the going rate, the lowest listing named a room at 14 (7.3%) and no room at 178, so its price alone does not say which room it buys. On the study's original room check, a lower listing whose room name matched the room at the going rate turned up at 11 of the 192. New Year's Eve: a room named at 9 of 181, a match at 3. Mid January: a room named at 17 of 169, a match at 14.

Compared with Thanksgiving week (exploratory)

Our Thanksgiving week page priced these same hotels a day earlier, on October 12, 2026, for Wednesday, November 25 to Sunday, November 29, 2026: the same weekdays as this Christmas stay. At the 220 hotels with a going rate for both, the median change from the Thanksgiving rate to the Christmas rate was 0.0% (95% interval -3.8% to 0.0%): the median hotel's rate was the same for both; higher at 92 hotels, lower at 109, the same at 19. The median going rate across all hotels with a price was $158 a night for Thanksgiving week and $148 for Christmas. The two were priced on different days and at different distances from the stay (44 days ahead for Thanksgiving, 71 for Christmas), so we describe this and do not test it.

What this shows, and what it does not

It shows what booking sites listed on Google Hotels on one day, October 13, 2026, for 225 hotels: 9 in each of 25 US cities. These are one day's prices, about ten weeks ahead; they will move. It is not a forecast and not every hotel: a sample this size has room for chance (each figure above says how much where it matters), the hotels are US only, and Google Hotels does not list every site. The going rate and the gaps are nightly rates averaged over the 4 nights, before taxes and fees as most sites list them; the hotel pages and the checker show prices all-in where the sites report taxes and fees.

The comparison week is mid January, not a typical week of the year, and it is not a perfect match for both holidays. It has the New Year's Eve stay's weekdays exactly; against Christmas it has a Tuesday night where Christmas has a Saturday night. It checks out on Saturday, January 16, the first day of the Martin Luther King Jr. Day long weekend, so its last night is that weekend's eve. It was priced further ahead (91 days) than Christmas (71) and New Year's Eve (77). A city's own events in any of the three weeks move its figure.

How much one city moves the two comparisons, a check we added after pricing: leaving out one city at a time, the New Year's Eve median stayed between +6.2% (without Honolulu) and +8.9% (without Washington DC), it stayed above zero every time, and it met the bar we set (p < 0.025) in 14 of the 25; the Christmas median stayed between -5.0% (without Minneapolis) and -2.5% (without New Orleans), it stayed below zero every time, and it met the bar we set in 8 of the 25. In two cities the January week itself was far pricier than either holiday, which pulls both comparisons toward lower holiday rates: Atlanta ($279 a night in January, against $119 for Christmas and $148 for New Year's Eve) and San Francisco ($678 a night in January, against $159 for Christmas and $183 for New Year's Eve).

Christmas and New Year's Eve hotel prices: common questions

Are hotels more expensive at Christmas?+

Not at the median hotel we priced: on October 13, 2026, the going rate for Christmas (December 23 to 27, 2026) was a median 3.9% lower than for a mid-January week ($5 a night less), at the same 211 hotels in 25 large US cities; lower at 119 of them, higher at 88, a difference within chance. These are one day's prices, about ten weeks ahead.

Are hotels more expensive on New Year's Eve?+

Yes at the median hotel we priced: on October 13, 2026, the going rate for New Year's Eve (December 29, 2026 to January 2, 2027) was a median 6.8% higher than for a mid-January week with the same weekdays ($10 a night more), at the same 207 hotels in 25 large US cities; higher at 118 of them, lower at 83. The difference met the bar we set before pricing, narrowly: with some single cities left out it would not. These are one day's prices, about ten weeks ahead.

When should I book a hotel for Christmas or New Year's Eve?+

This page cannot say when prices will be lowest: it is one day's prices, about ten weeks ahead, and they will move. On that day a booking site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 192 of 223 hotels for Christmas and 181 of 217 for New Year's Eve, so compare every site for your own hotel and dates before you book. If you book a rate you can cancel for free, Watch my booking checks the price once a day until your free cancellation ends and emails you once if a listed rate is at least 5% lower than what you paid (and at least $5 a night lower) on two daily checks in a row; you rebook yourself.

Is a lower holiday price on another booking site the same room?+

Often the listing does not say. At 178 of the 192 hotels with a listing 5% or more below the going rate for Christmas, the lowest listing named no room, so its price alone does not say which room it buys. Read the room and the cancellation terms on the booking site before you book.

Method and data

The same method and the same hotels as our Thanksgiving page (Thanksgiving week hotel prices, 2026), which follows the hotel price dispersion study with the same scoring code as its weekly snapshots. The 25 cities anchor 25 of the largest US hotel markets, each named as the study names its city there (Anaheim for the Anaheim and Santa Ana area, Honolulu for Oahu, Tampa for Tampa and St. Petersburg, Scottsdale for the Phoenix area, where the study has no Phoenix city). In each city we use the first 9 hotels, in Google's order, of that city's newest study snapshot that had a going rate there: the list the Thanksgiving page fixed before its first search, used here unchanged. The stays, the comparisons and the bar for a clear difference were written down before any search for this page. Each hotel was priced by its Google property token three times, for December 23 to 27, 2026, for December 29, 2026 to January 2, 2027 and for January 12 to 16, 2027, all on October 13, 2026 (UTC), for 2 adults, as a traveler in the US, in US dollars: 675 searches in all, one a hotel a stay. For each hotel and stay we kept every booking site Google Hotels listed. The going rate is the rate the largest number of listings agree on, with ties broken low; a lower listing counts when it is 5% or more below it; the room check asks whether the lowest listing names a room, and whether a lower listing's room name matches the room at the going rate. A hotel with no price for a stay is left out of that stay's figures and kept in the CSV with its price columns empty.

On November 3 and 4, 2026 we priced 100 of these hotels again, for Thanksgiving week and for Christmas week, to ask how often another site listed the stay below Booking.com: holiday hotel prices 2026, other sites against Booking.com.
