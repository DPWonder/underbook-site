How an alert decides when to write

Underbook's checker states its rule: "We check the going rate for this hotel and these dates once a day and email you if it is at least 5% lower on two daily checks in a row." Requiring two days in a row means a one-day blip does not send an email; it also means the email comes a day after the drop.

KAYAK, which also sends hotel price alerts, says: "Most Price Alerts are sent once a day." For its real-time alerts: "These trigger when there’s a significant price change, typically a 10% increase or drop, since your last update." Source: KAYAK help, Pricing and Price Alerts, read October 25, 2026.

A higher line sends fewer emails: in our daily series a 10% line fired at 6 hotels where a 5% line fired at 7. Trackers by name, what each watches and what each costs: hotel price trackers, side by side.

What an alert cannot do

It cannot lower a price. It tells you a price you watch has dropped; you pay less only by booking the lower rate and cancelling the first booking, which a non-refundable rate does not allow.

It follows a number, not a room. A drop in the going rate can come from a smaller room or a rate with stricter terms on one site, so read the room name and the cancellation line before you rebook.

It cannot see member prices. Our figures are public prices a signed-out visitor gets; a member or package price can sit below them. More on what happens after you book: hotel price dropped after you booked?

How to make a hotel price alert pay off

  1. Book a refundable rate you are happy with, and note its free-cancellation deadline.
  2. Set an alert for that hotel and those dates, on the site you booked with or with a tracker that checks every day.
  3. When it writes, compare like with like: the same room, the same cancellation terms, the total with taxes and fees.
  4. Book the lower rate first, then cancel the first booking before its deadline.

Hotel price alerts: common questions

Do hotel price alerts work?+

They catch real drops: run on our daily prices for 12 US hotels (one July 2026 stay, read once a day from June 3, 2026), a 5% drop alert would have fired at 7 of them. But the rate ended higher than on June 3 at 8 of the 12, so an alert helps only if your booking can be cancelled and rebooked.

How often does a hotel price drop within a week?+

Often enough to watch. Across 608 hotels priced twice a week apart in September and October 2026, the going rate fell 5% or more at 160 (26%) and rose 5% or more at 236 (39%).

How do hotel price alerts decide when to email?+

Each sets its own line. Underbook emails once when the going rate is at least 5% lower on two daily checks in a row. KAYAK says most of its alerts are sent once a day, and its real-time alerts trigger on a significant change, typically 10%, since your last update.

Are hotel price alerts reliable?+

An alert reports a price that was listed when it checked; the room behind it and the cancellation terms can differ from your booking. Open the lower rate, check the room and the total with taxes and fees, and only then rebook.

Can Underbook alert me if my hotel price drops?+

Yes, for free. Type the hotel and your dates into the price checker and add your email: it checks the going rate once a day across the booking sites and emails you when it is at least 5% lower on two daily checks in a row.
