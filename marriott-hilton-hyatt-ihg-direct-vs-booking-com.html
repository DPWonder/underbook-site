Measured, September 25 to October 1, 2026

Is it cheaper to book Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt or IHG direct?
Usually the same price as on Booking.com and Expedia.

Usually not cheaper, and usually the same: at 802 of 1,111 Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and IHG hotels (72%), the hotel's own site was within 1% of the lowest price on the six big booking sites (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline, Agoda and Trip.com), in our 14 price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026. Where the two differed by more than 1%, a booking site was lower at 214 (19%) and the hotel's own site at 95 (9%). Against Booking.com alone, the two were within 1% at 77% of 1,075 of these hotels; against Expedia alone, at 77% of 804.

Is it cheaper to book Marriott direct or on Expedia?

Usually neither: at 163 of 219 Marriott hotels (74%), Marriott's own site and Expedia were within 1% of each other, in our 14 price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026. Where they differed by more than 1%, Marriott's own site was lower at 31 (14%) and Expedia at 25 (11%). These are public prices; Marriott Bonvoy member prices need a sign-in and are not in these readings. For your own dates, check the hotel here: it lists what each site shows.

What it means for your booking

At each of the 8 chains, the hotel's own site was within 1% of the lowest big booking site at most of its hotels, from 67% at Accor to 80% at Hyatt. So at a big chain, booking direct usually costs the same as a booking site. Member and loyalty prices, which need a sign-in, are not in these readings.

Where the prices differed, it went both ways. Against Booking.com alone, the hotel's own site was more than 1% lower at 15% of 1,706 chain hotels and Booking.com at 8%. Against Expedia alone, the hotel's own site at 10% of 1,282 and Expedia at 12%. Against the lowest of all six big sites, the hotel's own site at 8% of 1,791 and one of the six at 20%: with six sites to look at, one of them was below the hotel more often than Booking.com or Expedia alone was.

So open the same room and dates on the hotel's own site and on the booking sites, read the total after taxes and fees on each, and take the lower one. How we counted

  • Source: the lowest public price each site listed on Google Hotels for a US searcher, in US dollars, for a two-night stay, in our 14 price snapshots read on September 25, 28, 29, 30 and October 1, 2026. Prices from one moment, not completed bookings.
  • "Like with like" means before tax against before tax and all-in against all-in, inferred from the hotel's two going rates; hotels where that could not be read are left out.
  • The hotel's own site is the seller Google Hotels lists under the hotel's exact name. "Within 1%" means neither price was more than 1% below the other; "lower" means more than 1% lower.
  • A chain hotel is one whose name carries a chain or brand name, the rule of our price study. Its chain here comes from the same name, by the brands listed with each chain above. The 14 Hilton Grand Vacations and Holiday Inn Club Vacations resort prices are in other chains.
  • The 1,791 chain hotels compared are 1,784 different hotels: a hotel priced in two snapshots counts twice. Percentages are whole numbers, so a row may add to 99 or 101.
  • Member and loyalty prices are not in it. Method, charts and the per-hotel CSVs: the hotel price study.
