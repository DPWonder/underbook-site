Hotel booking sites compared,
pair by pair.

Underbook is a free way to compare hotel prices across booking sites: its browser extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox and its web checker at underbook.co show, for your hotel and dates, the price each booking site lists, with taxes and fees as each site reports them (a resort or destination fee may be extra), and whether a lower price names the same room. No account is needed. This page is its own comparison of the big booking sites, from 16 price snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026 at 6,620 hotels in 368 cities. Short answer: the big booking sites usually landed close, and which one was lower depended on the hotel. In 12 of the 18 pairs below, the two prices were within $2 a night at more than half the hotels where both were on the same tax footing. Across all 18, that share ran from 20% (trivago and Booking.com) to 87% (Expedia and Hotels.com). So check two or three sites for your own dates, and compare the all-in total, not the headline rate.

Comparing hotel prices before booking for the holidays? Check the same hotel and dates on several sites and compare the totals: for Thanksgiving week 2026 (November 25 to 29, priced November 3, 2026), another site listed the same hotel and dates 5% or more below Booking.com at 74 of 76 hotels we compared, a median 22%, about $40 a night; for Christmas week (December 23 to 27, priced November 4, 2026), at 74 of 79.
