How the long-stay discount actually works

Hotels hate turnover: cleaning, check-in admin, and empty-night risk all shrink when one guest stays a week. So many properties will quietly sell a 5-night block cheaper per night than five separate nights. Stayforlong's model is to negotiate and surface exactly that inventory. The discount is real and it genuinely grows with stay length; on longer stays it can beat the mainstream sites, which mostly price per night and sit at parity with each other.

The tradeoff is flexibility. Deeply discounted long-stay rates are usually prepaid, and cancellation terms are stricter than a standard refundable OTA rate. That's also where Stayforlong's reputation takes its hits: when travelers need to change or cancel a prepaid week, a small support team plus strict terms makes for slow, frustrating outcomes. The math only works if your dates are genuinely locked. If they might move, price a refundable rate on a mainstream site or direct with the hotel and treat the gap as the cost of flexibility.

Before you book a prepaid long-stay rate, check three things

  • 1. The all-in total. Compare the price after taxes and fees, not the headline nightly rate.
  • 2. The room type. Make sure it's the same room you'd book direct, not a smaller or "run of house" version.
  • 3. The cancellation terms. A small difference isn't worth it if your plans might change and the rate is non-refundable.

Stayforlong, common questions

Is Stayforlong a scam?+

No. It's a real Barcelona-based OTA that has been operating since 2015. The complaints you'll find online are overwhelmingly about refunds and changes on prepaid rates handled by a small support team, not about fake bookings. Book it with locked dates and verified terms and the typical complaint doesn't apply to you.

Does Stayforlong only work for long stays?+

Its offers are built around stays of three nights or more, and its edge grows with length; a 3-night stay might see a modest discount while a week sees a meaningful one. It does list shorter stays: our price snapshots priced two-night stays, and Google Hotels showed a Stayforlong price at 634 hotels. For 1-2 night stays the long-stay discount has little to work with; use a mainstream comparison and check the hotel's own site.

Will the hotel honor my Stayforlong booking?+

In the normal case yes; the reservation reaches the hotel like any OTA booking. As with any discount channel, the practical risk is a mismatch on room type or refundability rather than a missing reservation. Confirming with the hotel a few days before arrival is a cheap sanity check on a long prepaid stay.

Can I cancel a Stayforlong booking?+

Check the specific rate: some are refundable within a window, but the deep discounts are typically prepaid and non-refundable, and processing goes through Stayforlong rather than the hotel. On a week-long prepaid stay that's real money, so read the cancellation terms twice before paying.

No. We have no affiliate relationship with Stayforlong. Underbook surfaces whichever site has the cheaper rate, says whether it names your room, and shows the lower price even when it earns nothing from it.
