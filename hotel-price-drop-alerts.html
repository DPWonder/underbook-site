How hotel price drop alerts work

Three steps. The only thing we need from you is an email to send the alert to.

1

Enter the hotel and dates

Type the hotel name and your dates into the free checker. It shows the rate on each booking site; add your email and Underbook starts watching the hotel's going rate for those dates.

2

We track it for you

Underbook re-checks the price on a schedule, and checks other booking sites for the same hotel and dates, not just the one you started from.

3

You get the drop alert

When the going rate is at least 5% lower on two daily checks in a row, you get one email with the new price and where it is. That is the only drop alert for that watch, and every email unsubscribes in one click. If your booking is refundable, that's your cue to rebook lower and cancel the old one.

When watching a hotel price helps

Hotel rates aren't fixed. A hotel repricing a room as its calendar fills or empties is normal, and it happens right up to check-in. The catch is timing: a price drop only helps if you find out while you can still act on it, before a fully-refundable rate's cancellation deadline passes. That's the whole point of a drop alert. You booked; you moved on; Underbook keeps watching.

And a "drop" isn't only the same site getting cheaper. The same hotel, same dates, is often listed for less on a booking site you didn't check (a different wholesale channel, less ad spend), though not always for the same room. Underbook watches for both, and when the room names don't match, it says to check before booking instead of pretending a lookalike is a match.

Hotel price tracking: common questions

Can I set a price drop alert for a hotel?+

Yes. Type the hotel and your dates into Underbook's free checker and it checks that hotel's rate for your dates once a day, then emails you when it drops at least 5% on two daily checks in a row. It's a hotel price tracker, similar to CamelCamelCamel for Amazon, but for hotels. No account or app needed to watch a price.

Do hotel prices really drop after I book?+

Often. Hotels reprice constantly based on occupancy, and many rooms are booked on fully-refundable rates. If the price falls before your cancellation deadline, you can rebook lower and cancel the old reservation. The alert is what gets you there in time. Here is the full what-to-do playbook when a price drops after you book.

Is it free?+

Yes. Watching a hotel price and getting drop alerts is free, no account required beyond an email to send the alert to. If you later book through a site Underbook surfaces, it may earn a small commission from that site; you pay nothing extra, and the commission never changes which rates it shows or their order.

How is this different from just checking myself?+

Underbook checks on a schedule so you don't have to remember, and it doesn't only track the site you booked on: it looks at other booking sites for the same hotel and dates, so a drop can also be another site listing the hotel for less. The email reminds you to check the room before you rebook. More on comparing the same room → If you're weighing the options, here's an honest look at which hotel price tracker fits your situation.

Is my data safe?+

The price check runs on Underbook's side from the hotel name and dates you type. It never sees or stores your payment details, booking happens on the site you choose, and it doesn't sell your data or email. Read the privacy policy →
