What Hilton agreed to, and what the law asks of everyone

The Nebraska Attorney General's office announced settlements on hidden hotel fees: "Hilton, Marriott, Omni, and Choice have committed to making their pricing practices more transparent." The chains agreed to "prominently disclose the total price of a hotel stay, including room rate and all other mandatory fees" on the first page of their booking websites, and "to include required fees when a consumer sorts by price". The release recalls that its office had sued Hilton in 2019. Source: the Attorney General's release, read October 23, 2026.

The FTC's rule makes the same thing the law for every seller of US hotel rooms, booking sites included: "Effective May 12, 2025, the FTC’s Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees, 16 C.F.R. Part 464, prohibits bait-and-switch pricing". A mandatory resort fee must be in the total shown first; "taxes or other government charges" may be left out of it and added before you pay. Source: FTC, Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees: Frequently Asked Questions, read October 23, 2026.

So the difference between Hilton and a booking site is not the rule but where each puts the fee and the tax. hilton.com answered our requests on October 23, 2026 with an error (HTTP 403), so this page does not describe how a given Hilton rate is laid out; read the total on its last screen as you would on any site.

One Hilton resort, on Booking.com

At the Hilton Hawaiian Village on October 19, 2026 (a stay of November 17 to 20, 2026, not signed in), all 85 offers on Booking.com listed the $55 nightly resort fee as included in the price, with the hotel's taxes on a separate line. Its fine print said the fee "may not always be added to your final online transaction": the hotel may collect it at the property instead. Expedia's page for the same hotel never loaded its rooms in two tries. More pages: which booking sites show resort fees upfront.

How to compare a Hilton price with the booking sites

  1. Take the total, not the first price, on Hilton's site and on each booking site, for the same room, dates and cancellation terms.
  2. Look for the resort fee in each total; on a booking site it can sit in the price, beside it, or in the fine print.
  3. Count what only one side gives you, such as a member rate or points on a direct booking, separately from the price.
  4. If a small site is much lower, check that its listing names the same room before you book.

Hilton prices and fees: common questions

Does Hilton show taxes and fees upfront?+

For mandatory fees, by its own agreement: Hilton agreed in a settlement with Nebraska's Attorney General to show the total price of a stay, including the room rate and all other mandatory fees, on the first page of its booking sites. Under the FTC rule in force since May 12, 2025, every US hotel and booking site must do the same, and taxes may still be added before you pay.

Is Hilton more transparent about price than the booking sites?+

Not by rule: since May 12, 2025 the FTC requires every seller of US hotel rooms to include mandatory fees such as a resort fee in the total it shows first. Where sites differ is where they put the fee and the tax. Compare the totals on the last screen.

Is Hilton cheaper than Booking.com or Expedia?+

Usually the same: Hilton's own site was within 1% of the lowest of six big booking sites at 262 of 372 Hilton hotels (70%) in our 16 price snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026, lower at 49 and higher at 61.

Do booking sites show a Hilton resort fee?+

They must show a mandatory resort fee in the US total. At the Hilton Hawaiian Village on October 19, 2026, Booking.com listed the $55 nightly resort fee as included in every offer, and its fine print said the hotel may collect it at the property.

How do I check a Hilton price across sites?+

Compare the total for the same room, dates and cancellation terms on Hilton's site and two booking sites, or type the hotel and dates into Underbook's free price checker, which lists each booking site's price on Google Hotels and the hotel's own site when Google shows it.
