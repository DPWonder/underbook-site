  • iPhone and iPad: the web price checker, in Safari or any other browser. Or, for the comparison on the hotel page you are viewing in Safari, Underbook for Safari on the App Store, free.
  • Android: the web price checker, in any browser. Underbook runs in Firefox for Android 142 or newer, from Firefox Add-ons, if you want the comparison on the hotel page you are viewing.
  • From the Booking.com app or any other app: note the hotel's name and your dates, type them in the form above, then compare the total, the room name and the cancellation terms.
  • What the check cannot do: it lists the sites Google Hotels lists for that stay, so a site Google Hotels does not list is not in it. A price can change between the check and the site, so read the total on the site before you pay. A resort or destination fee may be extra.

Common questions

How can I compare one hotel's price across booking sites on my phone without installing an app?+

Use a website in your phone's browser. Underbook's web price checker at underbook.co/hotel-price-checker takes one hotel and your dates and lists the price each booking site Google Hotels lists for that stay. Google Hotels itself also works in a phone browser.

Is there an iPhone app that compares one hotel across Booking.com, Expedia and Agoda?+

For one hotel you do not need one: the web price checker runs in Safari or any other browser on an iPhone. For the comparison on the hotel page you are viewing in Safari, get Underbook for Safari on the App Store, free, for iPhone and iPad. trivago has an App Store app, if you prefer one.

Can I use a hotel price comparison extension on my iPhone?+

Yes, in Safari: get Underbook for Safari on the App Store, free, then switch it on in Safari (the app shows how). It shows the comparison on the hotel page you are viewing. Chrome extensions do not run on an iPhone. Directo's site listed Safari on iPhone, in beta, when we read it in October 2026. Extensions compared. The web price checker needs no extension.

How do I compare hotel prices on an Android phone?+

In any browser, use the web price checker. Or, in Firefox, add the extension: Underbook runs in Firefox for Android 142 or newer, from Firefox Add-ons, and shows the comparison on the hotel page you are viewing.

I found a hotel in the Booking.com app. How do I check it on other sites?+

Note the hotel's name and your dates, type them in the form at the top of this page, and read the list. Then compare the total, the room name and the cancellation terms with what the app shows.

Does it book the hotel?+

No. Each site's button opens that site, and you book there. If you book through a site we link to, we may earn a commission, which never changes the order of prices.

Why might a check show no price?+

Some hotels and dates have no listings on Google Hotels, or the name does not match one hotel. The result says so and links a search for that hotel and those dates on Booking.com and Expedia.

Are taxes and fees included?+

Each price is all-in where the sites report taxes and fees, which they did at 4,062 of 5,830 hotels (70%) in our data; otherwise it is the listed rate, marked as such. A resort or destination fee may be extra.
