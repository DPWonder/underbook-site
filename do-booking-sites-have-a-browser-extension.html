What does the job instead

A booking site shows its own price. An extension from an independent maker can show the other sites' prices on the same page. Underbook is a free extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox that shows, on the booking page you are viewing, the price each booking site lists on Google Hotels for that hotel and dates, and whether a lower listing names the same room. That is every site Google Hotels lists for the stay, a median of 17 per hotel in our data.

  • Where it works: Booking.com, Expedia and its sister sites (such as Hotels.com, Hotwire, Orbitz and Travelocity), Agoda, Trip.com, Priceline, Marriott and Hilton, and many hotels' own websites.
  • Prices: taxes and fees as each site reports them; a resort or destination fee may be extra. Read the total on the site before you pay.
  • Other extensions: Hotel Ninja, Directo and RatePunk are compared with Underbook, each in its own words, on hotel price comparison extensions.
  • No install: the free web price checker runs the same check for one hotel and your dates, in any browser.
