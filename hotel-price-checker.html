How the check works

  • Every site Google Hotels lists for that hotel and dates, including the lesser-known ones where the lower prices usually are.
  • The going rate is the price most sites agree on. A site 5% or more below it is flagged as cheaper.
  • Same room or not. We compare the room each site names. "Same room name" only when the names match (strong evidence, not proof); otherwise we show you which room the lower price is for, so you can judge. Many cheap listings name no room at all. We measured how often, on 7,044 hotel prices.
  • All-in prices include taxes and fees as each site reports them; a resort or destination fee may be extra. When no site shows a before-tax rate, we say fees may be missing.
  • We never book anything and never see payment details. You book on the site you choose.
