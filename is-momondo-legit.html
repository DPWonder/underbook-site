Is momondo safe to book through?

momondo calls itself a travel metasearch site: it looks up prices on other sites and lines them up. Its About page says it does not sell flight tickets, hotel rooms or car rentals and is not a travel agency; KAYAK, which manages it, says the same of itself: as a travel search engine it does not sell or process your tickets or reservations. So you never pay momondo for a hotel. When you pick a price, momondo sends you to that site, and the booking is made there.

That makes the safety question a question about the site you land on. It may be the hotel's own site, a large booking site or a smaller one you have not heard of. Its terms decide the room you get, whether you can cancel, and who you call if something goes wrong. Before you pay, check three things on that site:

  • 1. Who it is. Find the company name in its footer or terms. We have pages on many of them: Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, Trip.com, and 43 lesser-known booking sites.
  • 2. The total. Compare the price on the last booking screen, taxes and fees as each site reports them; a resort or destination fee may be extra.
  • 3. The room and the terms. Check the room name and bed type, and read the cancellation line before you pay.

momondo works like its sister sites. We have the same breakdown for KAYAK, and for two other search sites, Skyscanner and trivago.

momondo: common questions

Is momondo legit?+

Yes. momondo is a travel search site founded in 2006 and based in Copenhagen. Its About page says it is managed by KAYAK, part of Booking Holdings Inc., and Booking Holdings lists momondo among its brands. It compares prices from other sites and does not sell rooms itself: you book and pay on the site it sends you to.

Is momondo safe?+

Searching on momondo is safe: it is free, it says it adds no booking fees, and you pay it nothing for a hotel. The booking itself is made on the site you click through to, so how safe that is depends on that site. Check who it is, the total on its last booking screen and its cancellation terms before you pay.

Who do I contact about a booking I found on momondo?+

The site you booked on. momondo says it is not a travel agency and the booking is completed on the site you chose, so your confirmation, changes and refunds come from that site under its terms.

Why can the price change when I click through from momondo?+

The price momondo shows comes from the other site, and that site sets the total at its own checkout. Its last booking screen may add taxes and fees (as each site reports them; a resort or destination fee may be extra), and a room can sell out or change price between your search and your click. Compare totals on the last booking screen, not the first number you saw.

Is momondo the same as KAYAK?+

They are separate sites with one manager: momondo's About page says it is managed by KAYAK, and Booking Holdings lists both among its brands. In our price data the two showed the same price at nearly every hotel where Google Hotels listed both (the figures are above), so checking both is not two separate checks.

Does Underbook get paid to recommend momondo?+

No. We have no affiliate relationship with momondo or KAYAK. This page isn't a referral pitch; it's an answer to a question a lot of travelers ask.
