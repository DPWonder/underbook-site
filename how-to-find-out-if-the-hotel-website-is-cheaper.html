The hotel's own site against the booking sites

  • Against the lowest of six big booking sites (Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline, Agoda, Trip.com) at 3,710 hotels: the hotel's site was within 1% at 48%, more than 1% lower at 17%, and a big site was lower at 35%.
  • At chain hotels the hotel's site was within 1% of the big sites at 72%. At independent hotels only at 20%: there the hotel's site was lower at 27% and a big site at 53%, while against Booking.com alone the hotel's site was lower more often (46% against 23%).
  • Against every listing, smaller resellers included, the hotel's site was at or below all others at 993 of 3,935 (25%) hotels; another listing was 5% or more lower at 1,766, and that listing named a room at only 377.

So check the hotel's own site every time, and against more than one booking site: at chains it usually matches them, at independent hotels it differs in either direction. A much lower reseller price is a lead to check, not proof of the same room.

Where the hotel's own price shows up

Google says: "Hotel prices come from Google partners like hotel owners, online travel agencies, metasearch engines, and other travel providers." So a hotel that sends its price is listed beside the booking sites; one that does not is not. Source: Google Travel Help, Search for hotels on Google, read October 27, 2026.

Booking.com says "The rates displayed on our Platform are set by the Service Providers." and that "Once your stay is finished, the Service Provider just pays us a commission." A booking on the hotel's own site carries no booking-site commission, which gives the hotel room to list its own rate at or below the one it gives a booking site. Source: Booking.com, How we work, read October 27, 2026.

Member prices are not in our figures: a hotel's member rate (and a Booking.com Genius price) needs signing in, and can be lower than the public price on either side.

How to find out, for your stay

  1. Search the hotel and dates on Google Hotels (or in the box above) and look for the hotel's own name among the sellers.
  2. If it is not listed, open the hotel's own site and search the same dates and room.
  3. Match the room and the cancellation terms, then compare totals with taxes and fees on both last screens.
  4. Check a member price on the hotel's site if joining is free; count points separately from the price.

Booking direct with the hotel: common questions

How do I find out if the hotel's own website is cheaper?+

Search the hotel and your dates on Google Hotels, which lists the hotel's own site beside the booking sites when it has that price (at 67% of hotel prices in our 16 snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026). Then compare totals for the same room and cancellation terms, on the hotel's site and the booking site.

Is it cheaper to book directly with the hotel?+

Often it is the same. Against the lowest of six big booking sites at 3,710 hotels, the hotel's own site was within 1% at 48%, lower at 17% and higher at 35%. Against Booking.com alone it was more than 2% lower at 26% of hotels and Booking.com at 13%.

Why would a hotel's own site be lower?+

A booking on the hotel's own site carries no booking-site commission. Booking.com says the provider pays it a commission once the stay is finished, so a hotel has room to price its own rate at or below the booking sites.

Does Google Hotels show the hotel's own website?+

When the hotel sends its price: Google says hotel prices come from partners like hotel owners, online travel agencies and metasearch engines. In our snapshots Google Hotels listed the hotel's own site at 4,425 of 6,620 hotel prices.
