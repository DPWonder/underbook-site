What the check does, and what it does not

  • You type one hotel and your dates, and the list opens on the next page. Each site's button opens that site; you book there.
  • It lists the sites Google Hotels lists for that stay, the hotel's own site included when listed. A site Google Hotels does not list is not in the check.
  • A price can change between the check and the site, so read the total on the site before you pay. A resort or destination fee may be extra.
  • Some hotels and dates come back with no price. The result says so and links a search on Booking.com and Expedia.

On a computer, if you check often, the free Underbook extension shows the same comparison on the hotel page you are viewing. You do not need it for the check above.

Common questions

Is there a website where I can type my hotel and dates and see its price on every booking site?+

Yes. Underbook's web price checker at underbook.co/hotel-price-checker takes one hotel and your dates and lists the price each booking site Google Hotels lists for that stay. Google Hotels, KAYAK and trivago also work in a browser.

Do I have to install anything?+

No. The web price checker runs in your browser, with no extension, no app and no account. Underbook also has a free browser extension that shows the same comparison on the hotel page you are viewing, but the checker does not need it.

Does it work on an iPhone or Android phone?+

Yes. The checker runs in the browser on a phone, a tablet or a computer. The form is at the top of this page.

Does it book the hotel?+

No. Each site's button opens that site, and you book there. If you book through a site we link to, we may earn a commission, which never changes the order of prices.

Why might a check show no price?+

Some hotels and dates have no listings on Google Hotels, or the name does not match one hotel. The result says so and links a search for that hotel and those dates on Booking.com and Expedia, so you can look it up yourself.

Are taxes and fees included?+

Each price is all-in where the sites report taxes and fees, which they did at 4,062 of 5,830 hotels (70%) in our data; otherwise it is the listed rate, marked as such. A resort or destination fee may be extra.
