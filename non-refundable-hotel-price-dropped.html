Non-refundable hotel, and the price dropped?
Rebooking won't help. A like-for-like price match might.

With a non-refundable rate you can't cancel for your money back, so the usual trick of rebooking and cancelling is off the table. What is left depends on where you booked and where the lower price is. What still works, by where you booked

The cheaper offer has to be the same stay with the same conditions, including being non-refundable.

Booked on Booking.com

Booking.com's We Price Match covers the same accommodation with the same conditions found for less on another website after you book, and the cancellation policy must be the same as yours. We did not see non-refundable bookings excluded, but confirm with customer service. Don't cancel: a cancelled booking can't be matched. How to claim.

Booked direct with Marriott or Hilton

Both programs need a lower rate on another site with the same cancellation terms, and a claim within 24 hours (Marriott counts from booking; Hilton's steps say within 24 hours). If you booked less than a day ago, look now. The rules for each chain.

Anywhere else, or too late

Read your booking site's own terms for a price-match policy, and look up whether it still runs one before you count on it. Otherwise, a polite ask to the hotel is the last option. It costs nothing, but nothing obliges them to say yes.

Making a price match claim on a non-refundable booking

  1. 1

    Find the same stay, also non-refundable. A refundable offer elsewhere is a different product, even at a lower price. Match the room, beds, guests, dates and meal plan. The price checker shows every booking site Google Hotels lists for your dates; open the site to confirm the room and the terms.

  2. 2

    Make sure it is an ordinary public price. Booking.com excludes prices lowered for logging in or using a coupon code, Marriott excludes rates behind paid memberships such as AAA, and Hilton excludes prices you need a login, a credit card number or a code to see.

  3. 3

    Capture it, then claim right away. Take a screenshot that shows the hotel, room, dates, guests, total and cancellation terms, and keep the link. Booking.com says the other offer must be online and available when it checks.

  4. 4

    Keep the booking. Cancelling a non-refundable booking doesn't return your money, and on Booking.com it ends your price match claim too.

Next time: price the flexibility before you skip it

Non-refundable is often the right call. But flexibility is sometimes cheaper than people think. In our 14 snapshots of 5,830 hotels (September 25 to October 1, 2026), at the 3,975 hotels that had both kinds of listing, the lowest-priced listing with free cancellation cost a median 9% more than the lowest-priced non-refundable one. At 1,535 of them (39%) the difference was 5% or less. And at 30% of hotels, no listing was marked with free cancellation at all, so sometimes there is no choice to make.

Non-refundable and cheaper now: common questions

Can I get a refund if my non-refundable hotel price drops?+

Not by cancelling: a non-refundable rate is not refunded when you cancel. What might work is a price match. Booking.com's We Price Match, and Marriott's and Hilton's price-match programs for direct bookings, compare offers with the same conditions, including the cancellation terms, so look for the same stay, also non-refundable, listed for less on another website, and check each program's rules before you claim.

Does Booking.com price match a non-refundable booking?+

Its terms require the other offer to have the same cancellation policy and conditions as your booking, along with the same property, accommodation type, dates, occupants and meal plan, found for less on another website after you book. So a non-refundable booking needs a non-refundable offer. We did not see non-refundable bookings excluded in that section, but ask customer service before you count on it. Do not cancel first: the terms say a cancelled booking can't be matched.

I booked a non-refundable rate direct with Marriott or Hilton. What can I do?+

If you booked less than 24 hours ago, look for the same room and terms on another website now. Marriott wants a claim within 24 hours of booking, Hilton says to submit yours within 24 hours, and both require the same cancellation terms. After that, a polite ask to the hotel is the remaining option.

Can Underbook watch a non-refundable booking?+

No. The booking watch is for stays you can cancel for free, because only those can be rebooked. For your next trip, compare the price of free cancellation before you skip it: in our data it cost 5% or less extra at 39% of the hotels that had both kinds of listing.
