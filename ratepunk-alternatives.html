RatePunk alternatives
Free extensions that compare hotel prices on the page.

Updated November 13, 2026: Underbook is a free alternative to RatePunk that shows, on the hotel page you are viewing, the price each booking site Google Hotels lists for your dates (a median of 17 per hotel in our data), and says whether a lower price names the same room. It installs on Chrome, Edge and Firefox with no account. RatePunk, also free, adds cashback in its own coins and a check of other countries' prices, and its home page now leads with flight prices. Directo and Hotel Ninja are the other free extensions in this group.

When RatePunk may be enough

If you want cashback in its coins, a check of what other countries are charged or help with Airbnb stays, RatePunk says it does those, free. Underbook is the one to try if you want every site Google Hotels lists for your stay, small sites and the hotel's own site included, or want to be told whether a lower price names the same room. Both can email you when a refundable stay drops after you book.

What to look for in any of them

Three things from our hotel price study (7,044 hotel prices in 368 cities, 17 snapshots) that decide whether a price comparison is worth trusting:

  1. Which sites it checks. Some site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 5,114 of 7,044 hotels (73%), and the lowest of those listings was on Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Priceline or Agoda at only 641 of 5,114 (12.5%). Most lower prices sit on smaller sites, so the number of sites checked matters.
  2. What it says about the room. At 4,294 of 5,114 hotels (84%) with a lower listing, the lowest named no room at all. A tool can only match a room the listing names, so look for one that tells you when it cannot.
  3. Taxes. At 2,542 of 3,371 hotels (75%) where taxes added 10% or more (snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026), the listings mixed before-tax and all-in prices, and at each of those the lowest listing was a before-tax price. Compare all-in totals, or know which you are seeing.

RatePunk alternatives: common questions

What is a good alternative to RatePunk?+

Underbook, Directo and Hotel Ninja are free extensions that also compare a hotel's price across booking sites on the page you are viewing. Underbook installs on Chrome, Edge and Firefox, checks every booking site Google Hotels lists for your stay, says whether a lower price names the same room, and has Underbook for Safari, a free app for iPhone and iPad on the App Store since November 10, 2026 (iOS 16 or later) and a web price checker for other phones.

Is RatePunk free?+

Yes, by its Chrome Web Store listing and extension page, read November 8, 2026. It may earn a commission on bookings, its listing says. Underbook is free too and may also earn a commission if you book through its link.

Is RatePunk still updated?+

Its Chrome Web Store listing shows version 1.49.8, updated April 21, 2026, and its Firefox Add-ons listing was updated January 7, 2025, read November 8, 2026. Its home page now leads with flight prices; its extension page still describes hotels.

Does RatePunk work on Edge?+

Its site links Chrome, Safari and Firefox extensions and names no Edge one, read November 8, 2026. Underbook has a listing on Microsoft Edge Add-ons, as well as the Chrome Web Store and Firefox Add-ons.

Why try a smaller extension?+

Underbook has fewer users: 71 on Chrome and no ratings yet, against RatePunk's 50,000 and 3.7 from 133 ratings, read November 8, 2026. What it adds is a check of every site Google Hotels lists and a plain answer on whether the room matches. You can test that with no install at the web price checker, then decide.
