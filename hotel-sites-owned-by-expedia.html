How we measured: in each snapshot we recorded every booking site's price that Google Hotels showed a US searcher, in US dollars, for a two-night stay checking in between October 30 and November 5, 2026, at 5,830 hotels in 348 cities. For each hotel we took each site's lowest price, all-in with taxes and fees where the site reported them, otherwise its listed rate. Within $2 means the two differed by $2 a night or less; more than 2% cheaper means one was below 98% of the other. A price we saw is one moment, not a completed booking. Hotwire and trivago, the two that work differently

  • Hotwire is an Expedia Group company; its own terms are signed Hotwire, Inc., an Expedia Group company. Its Hot Rate hotels are sold without the name: the hotel's name and exact address are not shown until after you pay, and its rules say those bookings are final, with no changes or refunds. Hotwire did not appear as a seller on Google Hotels at any of the 5,830 hotels, so we cannot say how a Hot Rate compares with Expedia's price for the same hotel. More: is Hotwire legit?
  • trivago is a separate listed company whose annual report names Expedia Group as its majority shareholder. trivago compares other sites' prices and sends you to one of them; its booking site, trivago DEALS, sells rooms itself. More: trivago vs Expedia and trivago vs Hotels.com.
  • Outside the group. Booking.com, Priceline, Agoda and KAYAK belong to Booking Holdings, Expedia Group's rival, so they are a real second quote: Expedia vs Booking.com, Priceline vs Expedia, Agoda vs Expedia. So is Trip.com, which belongs to Trip.com Group, not Expedia: Trip.com vs Expedia and Trip.com vs Hotels.com.

Expedia's sites: common questions

Which hotel booking sites does Expedia own?+

Expedia Group lists Expedia, Hotels.com, Vrbo, Orbitz, Travelocity, CheapTickets, Hotwire, ebookers and Wotif among its brands, and it is trivago's majority shareholder. In our price snapshots of September 25 to October 1, 2026, Orbitz, Travelocity and CheapTickets showed the same price as Expedia at the 3,294 hotels where all four were listed; Hotels.com and trivago DEALS priced differently more often.

Does Expedia own Hotwire?+

Yes. Hotwire's own terms are signed "Hotwire, Inc., an Expedia Group company", and Expedia Group lists Hotwire among its brands. Hotwire's Hot Rate hotels hide the hotel's name and exact address until after you pay, and its rules say those bookings are final. Hotwire did not appear on Google Hotels at any of the 5,830 hotels in our snapshots, so we could not compare its prices with Expedia's.

Does Expedia own trivago?+

Expedia Group is trivago's majority shareholder, according to trivago's own annual report, and lists trivago among its brands. trivago compares other sites' prices; its booking site, trivago DEALS, set its own prices in our data.

Are Orbitz, Travelocity and CheapTickets cheaper than Expedia?+

Not in our data. Orbitz showed the same price as Expedia.com at all 3,317 hotels listing both, Travelocity at all 3,372, and CheapTickets was within $2 of it at all 3,514 (the same price at 3,510). In our data, checking all four for one hotel gave one price, not four.

Is Hotels.com cheaper than Expedia?+

Sometimes. At the 3,542 hotels listing both, their lowest prices were within $2 a night at 84%. When one was more than 2% cheaper it was Hotels.com at 523 hotels and Expedia at 58.

Is Hotwire cheaper than Expedia?+

We cannot say from our data: Hotwire's Hot Rates were not listed on Google Hotels, where our prices come from. A Hot Rate hides the hotel until you pay and cannot be refunded, so compare its all-in total, room and terms with a named rate for the same dates before you book it.
