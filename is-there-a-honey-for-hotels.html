What a lower hotel price usually leaves out

A coupon code takes a known amount off one site's price. A lower price on another booking site is a different kind of lead: in our 16 price snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026 (6,620 hotel prices, a median of 12 booking sites per hotel):

  • Some site listed the stay 5% or more below the going rate at 4,783 of 6,620 (72%), typically 17% below.
  • That lower listing named a room at 775 of 4,783 (16%), and named the same room as a listing at the going rate at 571 of 4,783 (12%).
  • Sellers report taxes differently: where taxes added 10% or more, KAYAK's listed price on Google Hotels sat near the before-tax rate at 37% of hotels, Booking.com's at 8%.

So the check that matters for a hotel is not a code but the room and the total: open the lower listing, match the room name and the cancellation terms, and compare totals on the last screen.

Each snapshot: the lowest public price each booking site listed on Google Hotels for a US searcher, in US dollars, for a two-night stay, at one moment; not completed bookings, and not member or signed-in prices. Honey and hotel prices: common questions

Is there something like Honey for hotel prices?+

Not Honey itself: its own pages, read October 27, 2026, describe coupon codes at checkout (booking travel included), a price-drop list for items at one store, and seller comparison on Amazon, but not comparing one hotel's price across booking sites. Free extensions that do that on the hotel page you are viewing include Hotel Ninja, Directo, RatePunk and Underbook.

Does Honey find hotel coupon codes?+

Honey's home page says it looks for coupons when you shop on thousands of sites, booking travel among them, and its FAQ says hotel and other travel reservations earn rewards points on a different timeline. A code, when there is one, applies to that one site's price.

Can Honey's Droplist watch a hotel price?+

Honey says that if it detects a price drop at that store, it will notify you. For one hotel and dates across booking sites, the trackers that email you are Google Hotels, KAYAK's Price Alerts, trivago's app and Underbook's free price checker.

Does Underbook find coupon codes?+

No. Underbook compares the price each booking site lists on Google Hotels for your hotel and dates, all-in where the sites report taxes and fees (a resort or destination fee may be extra), and says whether a lower listing names the same room.

Is the lower price another site shows the same room?+

Often it names no room: in our 16 snapshots of September 25 to October 10, 2026, a listing 5% or more below the going rate named its room at 775 of 4,783 hotel prices (16%). Open it and match the room before you book.
