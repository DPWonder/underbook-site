Is Algotels legit?
Yes. Real rates, real tradeoffs.

Yes, Algotels is legit: a discount hotel site built by Holisto, a travel-technology company that resells rooms sourced from wholesale supply channels. Algotels is a Holisto brand; its site footer now reads "Algotels by trivago DEALS Ltd.", and Holisto's own site has stopped taking new bookings (both read September 28, 2026). Resellers of this kind are often the lowest-priced listing for a hotel: in our September 2026 study (6,254 hotel prices), one of them, Super.com, was the lowest-priced site most often, at 1,239 hotels, typically 19% under the going rate. Algotels itself did not show up on Google Hotels in that data (details below), so you will usually have to look up its price on Algotels directly. The rooms are real and bookings go through. The tradeoffs are the usual wholesale ones: prepaid, strict cancellation, thinner support, and a rate that deserves verification before you pay. Here's the honest breakdown.

Why is it so much cheaper than other sites?

The answer is boring, not shady. Hotels release inventory to wholesale distribution channels at a discount to keep rooms full, and technology resellers like Algotels pass part of that discount on to win your booking. They also spend far less on brand advertising than the household names. In our September 2026 study, a reseller of this class, Super.com, was the lowest-priced site most often (1,239 hotels, typically 19% below the going rate), and the prices were real listings, not bait.

Here's the honest catch from that same study: Super.com named the room at none of the 1,239 hotels where it had the lowest price, and across all sites the lowest-priced listing named no room at 3,765 of the 4,512 hotels with a cheaper listing. That doesn't mean the price is fake; it may be a real rate with caveats (non-refundable, or a different room class). It means the burden of verification is on you: confirm the room type, the all-in total, and the cancellation terms before paying. We wrote up the full pattern in our write-up on whether the same room is cheaper elsewhere.

Before you book a prepaid wholesale rate, check three things

  • 1. The all-in total. Compare the price after taxes and fees, not the headline nightly rate.
  • 2. The room type. Make sure it's the same room you'd book direct, not a smaller or "run of house" version.
  • 3. The cancellation terms. A small difference isn't worth it if your plans might change and the rate is non-refundable.

Algotels, common questions

Is Algotels a scam?+

No. It's a real discounter, a Holisto brand now under trivago DEALS, and the reservations go through. The complaints you'll find online cluster around terms and support: non-refundable rates, slow responses when plans change, and occasional room-type mismatches at check-in. That's the standard wholesale-reseller tradeoff profile, not fraud. Verify before you pay and the risk drops sharply.

Will the hotel honor my Algotels booking?+

In the normal case, yes; the reservation reaches the hotel through the wholesale channel like any other. The practical risk isn't a nonexistent booking, it's a mismatch between what you thought you bought and what the rate actually was (room class, bed type, refundability). Confirming the reservation with the hotel a few days before arrival is a cheap way to buy peace of mind.

Is Algotels cheaper than Booking.com or Expedia?+

On the headline number, it can be; wholesale-style rates like Super.com's were typically 19% below the going rate where they had the lowest price, and the big OTAs do not always match each other. Whether it's cheaper after taxes, fees, and the flexibility you give up is the real question, and that one you answer by comparing all-in totals for the same room, not search-result headlines.

Can I cancel an Algotels booking?+

Usually not without losing money; the lowest rates are prepaid and non-refundable, and any exceptions go through Algotels' support rather than the hotel. If your plans might move, a slightly more expensive refundable rate on a mainstream site or direct with the hotel is often the better buy.
